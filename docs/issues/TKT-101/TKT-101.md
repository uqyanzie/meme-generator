# TKT-101: Meme Creation Permitted After Subscription Expiration

- **Execution Order:** 4
- **Layer:** Backend
- **Status:** Open
- **Related Tickets:** [TKT-102](../TKT-102/TKT-102.md), [TKT-104](../TKT-104/TKT-104.md)

---

## Summary

Users can continue uploading base images and rendering memes after their subscription has expired.

## Reported Behavior

A user purchased a 1-day Premium package at 12:00 PM the previous day. Nearly one hour past expiration, the user was still able to upload and render memes. Restarting the browser briefly restored normal (blocked) behavior, after which meme creation became permitted again.

## Expected Behavior

Once a subscription's `ends_at` timestamp has passed, all meme creation endpoints and subscription status queries must immediately and consistently treat the subscription as expired and reject meme creation requests.

## Potential Impact

Revenue leakage and inconsistent access enforcement across sessions and clients.

## Initial Codebase Touchpoints

- `api/internal/modules/subscription/repository.go` (`ActiveByUser` checks `status = 'active'` without `ends_at > now()`, relying on the 1-hour `expireJanitor` in `api/internal/app/app.go`).
- `api/internal/modules/subscription/service.go` (`Status` calculates `MemesUsed` window).
- `api/internal/server/v1/v1.go` (`subscriptionGate.CanCreate`).
