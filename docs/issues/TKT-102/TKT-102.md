# TKT-102: Inconsistent Subscription Status and Cross-User Data Leakage

- **Execution Order:** 2
- **Layer:** Backend
- **Status:** Resolved
- **Related Tickets:** [TKT-101](../TKT-101/TKT-101.md), [TKT-104](../TKT-104/TKT-104.md)

---

## Summary

Subscription status updates inconsistently after payment, and authenticated user subscription/quota data is exposed to unauthenticated users on the same network.

## Reported Behavior

1. After completing payment and receiving a success confirmation, the Packages page continues to display an unsubscribed status, even after closing and reopening the tab.
2. Another user on the same office Wi-Fi network who had not logged in observed the first user's subscription status and quota on their screen.

## Expected Behavior

Subscription status is per-user authenticated data and must never be cached by shared caches (CDNs/proxies) or served stale from browser HTTP caches across users or post-payment mutations.

## Potential Impact

Critical privacy/security vulnerability (cross-user data leakage) and broken post-purchase UX.

---

## Initial Codebase Touchpoints

- `api/internal/modules/subscription/mount.go` (`GET /api/v1/subscription/status` sets `Cache-Control: public, max-age=86400`).
- Audit other authenticated endpoints (`auth/me`, `memes`, etc.) to ensure no other per-user route emits public cache headers.

---

## Pending Decisions (Discuss Before Implementation)

- [x] **Cache headers standard:**
  - **Header policy:** Use `Cache-Control: private, no-store` plus `Vary: Cookie` and `Vary: Authorization` (appended via `Header.Add` so `middleware.CORS`'s `Vary: Origin` is preserved).
  - **Enforcement scope:** Enforce globally at the entry of `middleware.Auth` for all authenticated endpoints (including `401 Unauthorized` responses), and remove the buggy inline `Cache-Control: public, max-age=86400` wrapper from `GET /api/v1/subscription/status` in `api/internal/modules/subscription/mount.go`.
